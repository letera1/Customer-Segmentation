# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors and participants in the Customer Segmentation ML Platform project.

## Expected Behavior
- **Respect and Professionalism**: Treat all team members, contributors, and users with courtesy, fairness, and constructive communication.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer and gracefully accept constructive feedback on code, architecture, and design.
- **Focus on Quality**: Prioritize clear documentation, reproducible bugs, and clean code that benefits the broader community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Demeaning, discriminatory, harassing, or exclusionary language and actions.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Unprofessional or disruptive behavior in issue trackers, discussions, or pull requests.

## Reporting and Enforcement
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project maintainers via the repository issue tracker or designated project email. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and take appropriate actions, including warnings or removal from repository participation.
